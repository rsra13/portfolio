The situation

In 2019, a global technology services firm realigned its High-Tech vertical under new leadership. The opening strategy was breadth: cover every sub-segment with every available offering, and the annual planning framework I designed tracked trends across multiple industries at once. Execution was rigorous, and the 2020 result was still flat revenue growth. The data exposed the root cause: resources spread too thin across divergent customer needs diluted sales effectiveness and weakened the value proposition - present everywhere, dominant nowhere.

What I did

For the 2021 planning cycle, I partnered with the Managing Director to diagnose the root cause and pivot the strategy from breadth to depth.

  • Strategic diagnosis: led a cross-functional analysis across Sales, Marketing, and Solutions to identify which ideal client profiles (ICPs) actually yielded profitable, repeatable engagements - and which ones consumed capacity without return.
  • Model pivot: redesigned the operating plan around the focused ICPs, consolidating offerings, strengthening specialized sales and marketing teams for high-yield segments, and exiting low-margin pursuits.
  • Operating rhythm enforcement: implemented stricter governance forums (QBRs and MBRs) so the team stayed aligned to the new focus, with decision logs that forced explicit trade-offs whenever a request fell outside the defined ICPs. Time-to-decision on critical escalations fell 40% under the structured governance, and leadership gained 100% visibility into portfolio risks and financials through a single dashboard.
  • Executive advisory: served as the trusted thought partner to the MD, translating ambiguous market signals into clear, actionable priorities for the leadership team.

The result

  • Revenue impact: double-digit revenue growth in the subsequent two years (2021-2022) by shifting from a scattered coverage model to a focused ICP strategy.
  • Market expansion: 30+ new logos secured during this period, validating the strength of the refined positioning.
  • Operational efficiency: wasted sales cycles reduced by eliminating non-ICP pursuits, letting delivery and solutions teams concentrate on high-value accounts.

M&A integration Value realization Change management

The situation

A major enterprise client ran a fragmented vendor landscape: redundant costs, inconsistent service levels, and missed revenue opportunities. Leadership chose consolidation to improve margins and client stickiness - but previous attempts had failed on poor change management and no synergy tracking, so this one had to be run differently from day one.

What I did

I led the end-to-end integration strategy, moving beyond simple contract termination to value realization.

  • Due diligence & modeling: conducted deep-dive analysis of every incumbent vendor and modeled the financial trade-offs, identifying synergies in pricing, SLAs, and technical capabilities, and assessing organizational risks including talent retention and client continuity.
  • Integration planning: developed a phased roadmap that balanced speed with stability, defined clear ownership for each workstream (Legal, Procurement, Delivery, Client Success), and established post-close governance to track benefits realization.
  • Change management: led stakeholder alignment across Sales, Finance, and Delivery, securing executive buy-in with a business case that tied vendor consolidation directly to margin improvement and client satisfaction scores.

The result

  • Revenue impact: 40% revenue growth within the consolidated portfolio by upselling expanded service offerings enabled by the new vendor partners.
  • Cost efficiency: redundancy eliminated and gross margins improved significantly, with strict compliance standards maintained throughout.
  • Operational resilience: 50+ enterprise accounts migrated without service disruption, preserving client trust and retaining key delivery talent through the transition - all without formal authority over any contributing team.

0-to-1 build Defect reduction Operating model

The situation

Specialists were assigned to projects reactively, often based on who escalated loudest. The allocation failure caused delivery delays, project attrition sat at 8%, and time-to-fill ran two months across a 50+ account portfolio serving an 800-person organization.

What I did

Designed and stood up the Talent Management Office as a 0-to-1 governance framework, run as a defect-reduction program where every delay, misallocation, and resignation was a defect to design out.

  • Framework design: defined roles, decision rights, executive scorecards, and weekly operating cadences, replacing ad-hoc assignments with structured planning.
  • Process standardization: created a centralized intake process that matched specialist skills to project requirements before commitments were made.
  • Continuous improvement: monitored utilization rates and attrition drivers, iterating the model until it became routine - then ran it until it was boring.

The result

  • Speed: time-to-fill fell from two months to under two weeks, a 75% reduction.
  • Retention: project attrition fell from 8% to under 3%.
  • Scale: the framework became the repeatable operating model for the entire 800-person vertical, stabilizing delivery quality across all enterprise accounts.

The situation

Strategy and operations teams were spending 30-40% of their time manually synthesizing data from disparate sources - CRM, ERP, and BI tools - to prepare for QBRs and board meetings. Manual assembly slowed insight generation and introduced human error into reporting accuracy. Leadership needed faster, more reliable, data-driven decisions.

What I did

I architected and deployed custom AI agents in Microsoft Copilot Studio to automate the data-to-insight pipeline. Proprietary code stays off this page, so what follows is the system architecture and the workflow design around it - the five-step pipeline below is the actual architecture, clickable step by step.

  • Workflow design: built agents that ingested raw performance data, applied natural-language synthesis to summarize trends, and flagged anomalies based on predefined thresholds.
  • Human-in-the-loop validation: designed a review mechanism where AI-generated drafts were validated by subject-matter experts before distribution, ensuring zero-defect accuracy for executive audiences.
  • Integration: connected the AI outputs directly into the existing PowerPoint and Excel reporting frameworks, letting analysts focus on interpretation instead of assembly.

The result

  • Speed to insight: QBR preparation accelerated from two weeks to three days.
