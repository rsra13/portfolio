01 · Selected work

Four builds I would run again tomorrow

Each one started as a problem nobody owned. The numbers come from delivered work; client organizations stay generic out of respect for confidentiality.

0-to-1 build Governance Operating model

The situation

Specialists were assigned to projects by whoever escalated loudest. Allocation failures caused delivery delays, project attrition sat at 8%, and time-to-fill ran two months across a 50+ account portfolio.

What I did

Designed and stood up the Talent Management Office as a 0-to-1 governance framework: defined roles, decision rights, executive scorecards, and weekly operating cadences. Then ran it until it was boring.

The result

Time-to-fill fell from two months to under two weeks. Project attrition fell from 8% to under 3%. The framework became the repeatable operating model for the entire 800-person vertical.

Microsoft Copilot Studio Executive reporting Automation

The situation

Recurring data pulls, market research synthesis, and status rollups consumed days of leadership time every month, and the inputs arrived messy from every direction of an 800-person matrixed organization.

What I did

Built and deployed custom AI agents in Microsoft Copilot Studio to automate market research synthesis, executive reporting, and lightweight tracking of priorities and decisions. AI became the default first step for problem-solving before escalating to humans.

The result

Reporting overhead dropped 30%. Quality stayed human by design: output validation and quality control remained manual, so nothing agent-drafted moved without a review pass.

The situation

What I did

Designed and ran the annual-to-quarterly-to-monthly planning cadence: operating plans with Finance, portfolio reviews with Sales and Delivery leads, execution checkpoints with ops teams. Set targets, tracked KPIs, flagged gaps, and escalated tradeoffs to the Managing Director with prioritized recommendations, on multi-year roadmaps aligning 50+ concurrent engagements.

The result

The cadence became how the vertical runs. Revenue, demand, capacity, and staffing forecast models hit 98% accuracy behind annual and multi-year investment decisions, and escalations arrived as prioritized options instead of fires.
